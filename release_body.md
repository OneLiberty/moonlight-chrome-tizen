# Instructions for Installing Moonlight on Your TV:

- Ensure that your TV is running on Tizen version 5.5 or higher.
- For a persistent installation, follow the instructions in the [README.md](https://github.com/OneLiberty/moonlight-chrome-tizen?tab=readme-ov-file#getting-started), which requires Docker. 
- Alternatively, use the MoonlightUSB.zip:
  - Download MoonlightUSB.zip.
  - Extract it to the root directory of your USB key:
    ```
    root/
    ├── userwidget/
        ├── app.tmg
        ├── widget.license 
    ```
  - Insert the USB into your TV and wait for installation.

> **Note**: Docker installation is more reliable than the USB method.
